What carries over

Different look.
Same foundation.

The styling changes completely between themes. These do not.

Built into every one

Phone, tablet and desktop

Designed at 390px first, then opened up — not squeezed down afterwards.

A hand-tuned dark mode

Each palette has a second version designed for it, rather than an inverted filter.

Keyboard and screen-reader support

Skip links, visible focus, labelled controls, and a working tab order.

Reduced-motion respect

Reduced-motion preferences are respected, with controls for background video.

Fast on cell data

Lightweight code, lazy-loaded supporting imagery, and no tracking scripts.

Scoped separately

Online payments and carts

Possible, priced on its own, and usually worth delaying until there is demand.

Customer logins

Rarely needed by a small business, and expensive to maintain badly.

Live booking calendars

Connected to a tool you already pay for rather than rebuilt from scratch.

Blogs and content libraries

Worth it only if somebody is genuinely going to write.

Automation

Form routing, reminders, spreadsheet and CRM handoffs. Checked against your tools first.

None of these are off the table. They are quoted separately so you are not paying for something you may never switch on.
