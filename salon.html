Good to know

Before you book

How do I know which appointment to book?

Book the consultation if you are unsure — it is free and it takes fifteen minutes. A real build can also recommend the right service and duration from a few questions.

Do you require a deposit?

For colour appointments over two hours, yes. It comes off the final bill and is refundable with forty-eight hours’ notice.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Always. Each profile books directly into that person’s calendar rather than a general queue.

What if I am unhappy with the result?

Tell us within seven days and we will put it right at no charge. It is rare, and it is far better than you living with it.

Do you do patch tests?

Required at least forty-eight hours before any colour service for new guests. A real build books the patch test automatically alongside the appointment.
