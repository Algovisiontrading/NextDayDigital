Questions

Asked at every kitchen table

How long does a roof take?

Most residential replacements are two to four working days, weather permitting. You will have the start and finish dates in writing before we order materials.

Do you require a deposit?

Not on roofing. Materials are ordered on our account and you pay on completion once you have walked it with the foreman.

What happens if you find rot?

The estimate already carries an agreed per-sheet rate for decking replacement, so there is no renegotiation halfway through. We photograph everything we replace.

Can you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document the damage, meet the adjuster on site, and bill the approved scope directly. You pay your deductible, not a dollar more.

How do I get an estimate?

Send the address, a description of the problem and a few photos. Most estimates are scheduled within three business days and written up the same visit.
