Ceramic coating

What the
warranty covers

Coatings are sold badly in this industry. Here is the plain version: a coating makes a car easier to clean and holds gloss longer. It does not make paint bulletproof.

Every coating we install is logged with batch number, installer and date, and registered with the manufacturer in your name. The warranty follows the car if you sell it.

Paint correction

How far to go

Clear coat is finite. Every correction removes a little, so the right answer is the least aggressive stage that reaches the finish you want.

Stage one

Gloss enhancement

One pass with a finishing polish. Lifts clarity and depth, leaves the heavier defects. Best on newer cars and lease returns.

Removes ~2–4µm

Stage two

Defect removal

Cutting pass followed by refinement. Takes out the wash marks and light swirls that make dark paint look grey in sunlight.

Removes ~5–8µm

Stage three

Multi-stage

Mapped panel by panel with a paint depth gauge. Reserved for cars where the finish matters more than the calendar.

Measured before every pass

Questions

Asked before every booking

Can you guarantee every scratch comes out?

No, and anyone who does is guessing. Deep scratches that catch a fingernail are through the clear coat. We will tell you which defects are coming out before any machine touches the car.

How long will the car be with you?

One day for a Reset, two to three for Enhancement, five to seven for Concours. Those are working days with the car indoors, not estimates that slip.

Do I need the five-year coating?

Usually not. It suits a garaged car you intend to keep. A daily driver parked outside is better served by the two-year system and an annual decontamination.

What about a new car from the dealer?

New does not mean flawless — most cars arrive with transport and dealer-prep marks. A single-stage polish before coating is almost always worth it.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes, up to seventy-two hours before the booking. Inside that window it covers the bay we held empty.
