Tax & compliance

Federal, state and local filings handled on a published calendar, with the documents we need requested six weeks ahead rather than six days. Extensions are a decision, not an accident.

Clean monthly books closed by the fifteenth, with a one-page summary written in sentences. If a number moved, you will be told why before you have to ask.

Engagement

How we start

Nothing is billed until an engagement letter is signed. You will know the fee before you commit.

Step 01

Introductory call

Twenty minutes, no charge. What the business does, what is not working, and whether we are the right fit.

Step 02

Document review

Last two returns and the current books. Returned within one week with what we found and what we would change.

Step 03

Engagement letter

Scope, responsibilities, deadlines and a fixed fee. Signed before any work begins.

Step 04

Working cadence

Monthly or quarterly, with a standing meeting in the calendar and a named contact who answers.

Questions

Before the first call

How are fees calculated?

A fixed monthly or annual fee agreed in the engagement letter, based on scope rather than hours. If the scope changes materially we agree a revised fee in writing before doing the work.

We already have an accountant. Is changing a hassle?

Less than most people expect. We request the handover file professionally, and the outgoing firm is obliged to provide it. Most transitions complete inside three weeks.

Do you work with businesses our size?

Our clients typically run between four hundred thousand and twelve million in revenue. Below that a bookkeeper plus annual return is usually the honest recommendation, and we will say so.

Will I actually speak to a partner?

Yes. The partner in your first meeting signs your work. There is no account-manager layer between you and the person who prepared it.

How do we send documents securely?

Through an encrypted portal, never email attachments. A real build links the public site to the firm’s approved portal rather than rebuilding one.
